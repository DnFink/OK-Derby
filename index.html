How To Play

Controls

  • A / D or ← / → Drive Left / Right
  • Space / W / ↑ Launch / Jump
  • W / S or ↑ / ↓ Fly Up / Down (during Lei Flight)
  • Shift Hold to Boost Speed (x1.5)
  • C or X Shoot Coconut Bombs
  • F or Z Shoot Pineapple Bubbles (High Arc)
  • Q Deploy Rescue Board/Boat (or Auto-Saves once per stage)
  • Esc Pause Game

Mechanics

Ocean & Lava Gaps: Build enough speed on ramps to jump over ocean and lava hazards.
Minions: Jump on top of beach crabs and bugs or blast them. Crashing into them is fatal!
Bosses: Defeat island bosses by shooting them or jumping on their heads!
I'iwi Bird: Defeating a boss frees a caged scarlet honeycreeper bird that flies you with a flower lei!
Tiki Spikes: Deadly spikes (urchins/bamboo) causing an instant wreck on contact. Jump over them!
Tiki Totems: Wooden barriers blocking movement. Stand on them or shoot twice to destroy them.
Tiki Fire Jets: Stone heads shooting periodic flame columns. Safe to pass when inactive.
Villain Competitor: Races you to the finish on every stage! Instead of fighting enemies, he drops time-bombs, spiked traps, and his own rival goons. Shoot or stomp him to stun him!
